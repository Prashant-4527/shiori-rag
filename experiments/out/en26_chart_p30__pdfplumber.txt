Growing Dependence on China for ASEAN Imports
(Emerging Economies Depend More on Chinese Intermediate Goods (iv))
• In the ASEAN countries, the proportion of intermediate goods items for which more than 50% of imports are
dependent on China has risen. China’s influence over the global value chain is significant, and depending on
the availability of alternatives, this could lead to supply chain risks such as supply chain disruptions. Rules are
crucial to reduce the supply chain risks and ensure the stability of trade.
